About Me

Fell in love with gaming at the age of 5. Started modding with the Source Engine at 16 and began prototyping and self-learning Unity Engine in 2013. Started building my own custom engine as a hobby in 2024, exploring rendering, engine architecture, and low-level systems.

Working professionally with game technology and environments since 2018, with experience across Unity, Unreal, Cocos2d-x, Cocos Creator, Construct 3, and custom engine environments. Worked across multiple platforms including Windows, Android, macOS, iOS, GDK, UWP, PS4, and PS5.

Experienced with C++, C#, Python, Java, and other programming languages, with a focus on graphics programming, engine development, automation, testing, debugging, and building scalable systems from the ground up.

Previously worked in the film industry as a producer. Outside of technology, I enjoy cooking, gaming, and exploring new ideas. Always happy to meet new people and pursue interesting challenges.
